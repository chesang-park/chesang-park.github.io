Research

My research focuses on developing efficient and generalizable robotic intelligence inspired by principles of human intelligence and behavior. I investigate how these principles can be incorporated into robotic policies to improve computational efficiency and generalization across diverse manipulation tasks. Specifically, I work on:
  • Adaptive Attention: Inspired by how humans selectively attend to relevant visual information, my research develops robotic policies that determine when and where to process visual inputs for computationally efficient decision-making.
  • Compliant Manipulation: Humans adapt their physical interactions to contact and environmental constraints. My research incorporates such compliance into robotic policies to enable robust contact-rich manipulation.
  • Hierarchical Intelligence: My research applies the way humans decompose complex tasks and reuse learned skills to structure robotic policies that select and compose reusable skills for scalable and generalizable behavior.
